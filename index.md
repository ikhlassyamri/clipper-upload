# clipper-upload

A **personal, single-user automation script** used by one individual creator to publish
their own short-form videos to their own accounts on YouTube, Instagram, and TikTok.

It has no other users: no sign-up, no login page, no distribution. Every video it
publishes is an original work created by its author, and it never accesses, downloads,
or republishes content belonging to anyone else.
